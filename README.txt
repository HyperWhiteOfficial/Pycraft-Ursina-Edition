--English--
How to play: 1 is to place a grass block. 
2 is stone, and 3 is brick. 
4 is bedrock, and 5 is cobblestone; 
6 is oak plank, and 7 is oak log; 
E is the inventory, which doesn’t work, and escape is the menu. 
In Minecraft Alpha Edition, grass is the right mouse button. 
The game requires Python 3.11.4 and installed Ursina
